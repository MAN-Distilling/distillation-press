The Cough

One cough. The smallest involuntary thing. And then — everything.

The Cough is a story about a man who thought he would not find out what he found out. About how the most minute details carry the largest ripples. About the moment before you know something, and the life you live after.

It is a story about how nothing that changes a life announces itself. It simply happens — quiet as a cough in an empty room — and the reverb goes on forever.
