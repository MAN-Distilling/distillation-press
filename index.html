The Press

An imprint built on conviction, not volume.

Distillation Press is an independent publishing imprint based in Vancouver, British Columbia. We publish a small number of titles each year — works chosen because they have something genuine to say and the craft to say it well.

We are not a vanity press and we are not a volume play. Every title on our list exists because it passed a simple test: would the world be worse without it?

The writers we work with come from different backgrounds, but they share a willingness to revise, to be honest, and to take the work seriously on its own terms.

"To publish work that has been genuinely reckoned with — by the writer, by careful readers, and by time — and to make it available to anyone who needs it."

How We Work

Small by design

A short list means every title gets full attention. We do not publish to fill a catalog. We publish when something is ready.

Accessible formats

Every title is made available in formats that work for the full range of readers — print, digital, and where possible, audio and accessible editions.

Print on demand

We use print-on-demand to keep books permanently in print without overproduction. Copies exist when they are needed, not as warehouse risk.

Writer-first terms

Writers retain meaningful control over their work. Our agreements are plain, our royalties are fair, and we do not lock authors into arrangements that no longer serve them.
